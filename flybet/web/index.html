            Why PAM11 / PPL101? Why only 15 cells?
            

              The simulation integrates all 166,700 retained neurons. Each
              circuit node summarizes a selected population; it is not an
              individual cell. The 3D view shows every cell with an annotated
              soma location.
            

            

              PAM11 and PPL101 are specific annotated cell types: this dataset
              contains 15 PAM11 cells and 2 PPL101 cells. Those counts describe
              these types, not all dopamine neurons.
            

            

              This experiment assigns gains to PAM11 stimulation and losses to
              PPL101 stimulation, alongside candidate plasticity at selected
              Kenyon-cell → MBON connections. These are modeling choices
              inherited from the Stonkfly/DOOMFLY framing, not established
              gambling circuits or a complete dopamine model.

        CONTROLLED LEARNING TEST
        
Did memory change the choice?

        

          Condition two visual patterns with reward and aversive pulses, then
          probe both without feedback. Each seed runs trained, frozen,
          erased-memory, and unpaired-feedback arms on the full connectome.
          Training is passive: no wagers or forced spins. Pattern meaning is
          counterbalanced across seeds.
        

        

          
          
          
          
        

        

          The decay interval applies the declared memory-decay equation, not an
          integrated period of brain activity. Probes freeze weights and reset
          electrical transients and chemistry so residual stimulation cannot
          masquerade as retained memory. All arms use the selected presets; the
          test controls learning independently of the slot memory selector.

        What this experiment measures +
        

          

            The official MaleCNS v1.0 wiring drives a simplified spiking model.
            A configured neural decoder proposes SPIN or WAIT and, in adaptive
            mode, a stake tier. No language model chooses actions. Three
            matching center symbols return 16 times the stake; other
            combinations return zero.
          

          

            Reward and pulse templates encode outcomes as currents into PAM11 or
            PPL101 cells. Chemistry templates can change sensitivity and add
            withdrawal drive. Actual spikes drive a candidate memory rule on
            existing KC → MBON07 / MBON11 connections. Weight changes alone do
            not establish learning, and this model does not measure pleasure,
            pain, or addiction.
          

          

            Each decision advances 300 ms of neural time, followed by 200 ms of
            feedback or neutral observation. Runs finish at their decision limit
            or when the fake bankroll cannot cover a stake. WAIT is a valid
            neural choice. The random baseline is explicitly separate from
            neural control.
          

          

            All scenarios use fixed, independent outcome probabilities. Changing
            scenarios starts a new run. The seed reproduces game randomness;
            exact neural reproducibility also depends on the recorded source,
            compiler, and model. Compare multiple runs and frozen-memory
            controls before interpreting behavior.
